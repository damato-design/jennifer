I’m a design systems and product designer. I turn scattered ideas into scalable, accessible foundations that teams actually want to use, and I make it look easy. _(Most of the time.)_

My path started in visual design and found its footing in product and systems work. Most days, that means a lot of tokens, a lot of documentation, and a lot of conversations about why the button looks the way it does. I care about both halves of the job: the craft, and the conversations that get a system adopted.

I have strong opinions, loosely held. I show up with a point of view, and I leave room for yours.

Accessibility isn’t a checklist for me. It’s the point. Good design works for everyone, including people who use screen readers, tab through interfaces, or need the contrast to actually be there. If a ratio is off by 0.1, I’ll notice. _(I always notice.)_

Outside of work, you’ll find me on the climbing wall, stomping through a DDR set _(that’s my cardio, and yes, I take it seriously)_, trying new restaurants, experimenting in the kitchen, or losing track of time in Random Dice.

[Let’s chat](https://cal.com/jennifer-damato). Tell me about your latest project, your favorite restaurant, or the design problem that’s been quietly haunting you. I’d love to hear it.
